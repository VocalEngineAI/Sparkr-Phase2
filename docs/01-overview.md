# Sparkr Phase 2 — Project Overview

## What this is

Sparkr currently gives restaurants, nail salons and spas a "One-Tab" landing page, but
businesses have no real tools to market themselves, work with influencers, or bring
customers back. **Phase 2 turns Sparkr into a full mobile ecosystem**, launching first in
New York City, made of four products sharing one backend:

| App | Platform | Users |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Business & Influencer App** | iOS + Android, one download, separate logins | Business owners/staff AND influencers |
| **Customer App** | iOS + Android, separate download | End customers of Sparkr businesses |
| **Admin Panel** | Web app | Sparkr's internal team |

All three connect to the existing One-Tab landing pages and share one backend so
campaigns, coins, deals, and messages stay in sync in real time.

## Client

- **Client**: Kenny Moy, Sparkr
- **Prepared by**: VocalEngineAI
- **Project type**: Business, customer and influencer mobile apps (iOS/Android) + web admin panel
- **Duration**: 5–6 months estimated (14–16 week dev minimum — see [05-timeline.md](05-timeline.md))
- **Launch market**: New York City first

## The problem (today)

- Influencer marketing is judged on views, not actual visits — no ROI tracking
- Google reviews are managed in a separate app from everything else
- No easy way to text or reward customers
- Customers can't find deals from Sparkr businesses in one place
- One-Tab page setup still runs on manual emails back and forth
- Little recurring revenue for Sparkr itself

## The solution

One mobile ecosystem for Sparkr businesses of any type — restaurants, nail salons,
and spas today, with no restriction baked in against other business types joining:
- A **business app** to run influencer campaigns, manage Google/social, message
  customers, sell vouchers/gift cards/tickets, and run a rewards/membership program
- A **customer app** to discover deals, join memberships, redeem rewards, book
  reservations, and buy event tickets
- An **influencer app** (same binary as business app, separate login) to find
  campaigns, apply, post, and get paid
- An **admin panel** for Sparkr staff to control every feature, approve profiles/pages,
  manage disputes, and track platform fee revenue

## Expected outcome

Businesses win measurable customers; Sparkr earns from campaign fees, voucher/gift-card
commission, 10% of ticket sales, messaging bundles, and paid add-ons.
