# Customer App — Features 17–19

## Feature 17 — Discover & Save

- **One Sparkr account** works across every participating business — not one app per business
- Discover deals, happy hours, vouchers, secret menus, rewards from all Sparkr businesses in one place
- Follow businesses; like, save, share their posts outside the app
- Wallet & membership cards: per-business coin balances, earn/redeem/expiry history
- Vouchers & bookings: members buy/redeem vouchers; any user can buy gift cards for friends, reserve, or join a waitlist
- **Not included**: food ordering/delivery, Sparkr holding wallet funds, customer posts or friend lists

### Wallet top-up (sub-feature)
- Members can top up a wallet for a business **only if that business opts in**
- Top-up packages, e.g. $100→+$10 reward, $200→+$25, $500→+$75, $1,000→+$200
- Payment via Apple Pay / Google Pay / credit card, straight to the business
- Business-only wallet balance; redeemed in-app at that business, balances update live
- **Not included**: Sparkr taking direct payment or holding funds, spending balance at other businesses

## Feature 18 — Business Profiles & Members-Only Offers

- Browse every Sparkr business, including ones not yet joined
- Business profile: posts, menu, photos, contact details, happy hours
- Offers/vouchers visible to everyone viewing the profile, but **redeemable only after becoming a member** (free, one-tap join)
- **Not included**: redeeming without membership, customer reviews inside Sparkr, businesses outside Sparkr

### Business profile page contents
- Overview: logo, name, category, location, price range ($–$$$$), description, map pin + distance, POS-style menu upload
- Info: phone, email, full address, hours, website (pulled from One-Tab)
- Social: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, RedNote icons linking out
- Happy hours & deals, promotions & events, promotional media
- Engagement: follow, save favorites, share profile, check in, add Memories photos, get directions, contact

### Memories, check-ins & leaderboard
- Memories wall: customers upload their own photos to a business's memory wall (social proof of real visits)
- Check-in button; other customers see total check-in count
- Top Visited leaderboard: ranks top visited/checked-in businesses over the past 30 days, on the customer dashboard

## Feature 19 — Interactive Map

- Every Sparkr business as a pin on a Google Maps-based map
- "Near me": location-based discovery, starting in NYC
- Filters: radius (5/10/25mi), city, business type, this week's specials/happy hours
- Tap a pin → see happy hours, deals, membership benefits; directions open in Google/Apple Maps
- From a pin: reserve or join waitlist (no membership needed), or become a member
- **Not included**: in-app turn-by-turn navigation, heat maps/live crowd levels, non-Sparkr businesses
