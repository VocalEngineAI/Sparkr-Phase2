# Timeline, Scope, Assumptions

## Process (5 stages)

1. **Discovery & Planning** — define goals, scope, roadmap. Locks open decisions
   (prices, free limits, cancellation fees, coupon rules) before design begins.
2. **Strategy & Design** — plan experience, functionality, architecture; UI/UX design
   for both apps + admin; clickable prototype.
3. **Development** — build and integrate to the approved plan. Runs in two-week
   sprints, demo at the end of each sprint, shared project board, weekly status update.
4. **Testing & Refinement** — QA, user acceptance, performance, security.
5. **Launch & Support** — store release, go-live, 45 days of post-launch support.

## Schedule

| Phase | Key activities | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery, Planning & Design | Lock decisions, flows, data model; design UI/UX; prototype | 3–4 weeks |
| Development | Backend, both mobile apps, admin, integrations | 14–16 weeks |
| Testing & Refinement | QA, UAT, performance, security | 2–3 weeks |
| Launch & Support | Store release, go-live + support | 1 week + 45 days support |

**Why not shorter**: scope is vast (three apps + admin panel + many integrations) —
development needs a minimum of 14 weeks. A 12-week total is not achievable.

## In scope / deliverables

**In scope**
- Customer app (iOS + Android)
- Business and influencer app (iOS + Android)
- Sparkr admin web panel
- Twilio, Stripe Connect, AI integration
- Google Business Profile + Maps APIs
- One-Tab and NFC integration
- Events and ticket sales

**Deliverables**
- UI/UX designs + clickable prototype
- Source code, docs, business user guide
- Apps live on App Store and Google Play
- Deployed admin panel
- QA report and launch checklist
- 45 days of post-launch support

## Assumptions, client responsibilities, out of scope

**Assumptions**
- Both apps are mobile (iOS + Android)
- Google Business Profile API gets approved
- Influencers post on their own social apps (not through Sparkr)
- Sales tax collected via Stripe or app stores

**Client responsibilities**
- Twilio, Stripe, Google, Meta, TikTok, and app store accounts
- Feedback within 2 business days
- Final prices, fees, and free limits (decided in discovery)

**Out of scope**
- POS and coin purchases
- Holding customer funds (escrow)
- Wallet top-up refunds
- Review/follow rewards (not possible per platform policy)
- Yelp review replies
- Third-party fees
