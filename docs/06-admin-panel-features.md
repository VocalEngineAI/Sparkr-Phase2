# Admin Panel — Features 24–26

Web app for the Sparkr team. Controls every feature across all apps.

## Feature 24 — Sparkr Admin Panel (core)

- Full feature control: every feature in business, customer and influencer apps managed from admin
- Announcement carousel: post announcements at the top of any dashboard (business/customer/influencer)
- Featured marketing: manage featured campaigns and sponsored posts in each dashboard's carousel
- Disputes & moderation: review disputes raised by businesses/influencers, plus campaigns, posts, vouchers, events
- Reports & business emails: cross-app reports with analytics and invoices emailed to businesses automatically
- **Not included**: accounting/tax software sync, custom report builder, third-party CRM integration

## Feature 25 — Coupons, Approvals & Payment Tracking

- Coupon generator: percentage or fixed amount (e.g. $50) codes for businesses
- Limits & expiry: one-time or capped uses, expiry date, one or several target businesses
- Coupons only work on features that cost Sparkr nothing (e.g. featured posts) — **not** SMS, MMS, or AI reviews
- Approvals queue: admin approves influencer profiles and One-Tab pages from one place
- Unpaid campaign alerts: paid campaigns not paid from wallet within 72 hours are flagged; admin can remind, disable, or ban
- **Not included**: coupons for SMS/MMS/AI reviews, Sparkr guaranteeing payments, Sparkr-handled refunds

## Feature 26 — Payments & Platform Fees

- Stripe Connect accounts for businesses and influencers; voucher/gift-card/ticket sales go to the business
- Fee structure:
  - Vouchers & gift cards: default **5%** for Sparkr (adjustable)
  - Event tickets: **10%** of every ticket sale
  - Paid influencer campaigns: **10%** (under $100), **15%** ($100–300), **20%** (over $300) deducted from wallet payment; **barter is free**
- Business wallet: non-refundable top-up to pay for campaigns, add-ons, SMS bundles, AI features
- Automatic invoices: every top-up, wallet spend, and charge recorded and invoiced automatically
- **Not included**: refunds of wallet top-ups, Sparkr holding customer funds, buying coins with money, tax filing for businesses
