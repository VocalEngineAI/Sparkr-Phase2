# Business Model — Revenue for Sparkr

| Revenue stream | Fee / mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| Paid influencer campaigns | 10% (under $100) / 15% ($100–300) / 20% (over $300) of wallet payment, by campaign size. Barter is free. |
| Featured/sponsored campaigns | Paid add-on (no fee tied to size) |
| Vouchers | Default 5% of each sale, adjustable |
| Gift cards | Small commission, e.g. 5% |
| Event tickets | 10% of all ticket sales |
| Sponsored business posts | 1/3/7-day paid rotation slots (posting itself is free) |
| Sponsored happy hours & deals | Paid featured placement (posting itself is free) |
| Special reservations | $0.25 per guest |
| Google management & reviews | Part of paid feature set |
| Social media management | Part of paid feature set |
| Sparkr One-Tab page add-on | Paid add-on |
| SMS & MMS | Bundles (500 or 1,000 messages) or pay-per-message |
| Advanced analytics | Paid feature, pricing TBD |

## Money flow principles (consistent across every feature)

- **Sparkr never holds customer or business funds.** Voucher, gift card, and ticket
  payments go directly to the business via Stripe Connect; Sparkr's cut is deducted at
  settlement, not held in escrow.
- **The business wallet is the one exception**: businesses top up a non-refundable
  wallet specifically to pay for campaigns, add-ons, SMS bundles, and AI features.
  Every top-up/spend/charge is auto-invoiced.
- **Refunds are never Sparkr's responsibility** — wallet top-ups, vouchers, gift cards,
  and tickets are explicitly "not included" for Sparkr-handled refunds across every
  feature doc. The business manages refunds/disputes with its own customers.
