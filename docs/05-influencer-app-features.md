# Influencer App — Features 20–23

Same app binary as the Business App, under the influencer login.

## Feature 20 — Sign-Up & Verification

- Link accounts: at least one of Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, RedNote at sign-up; can add more later
- Follower counts: influencer self-reports per linked account; **businesses verify it themselves**
- Admin approval: Sparkr admin reviews/approves or rejects each profile before use
- Verified platforms only: influencers only see/apply for campaigns on platforms Sparkr has verified for them
- Businesses find influencers by filtering: platform, followers, location (US/Canada state or province), past collaborations, social links
- **Not included**: fetching follower counts from APIs automatically, AI influencer picks for businesses, approving businesses (not needed)

## Feature 21 — Home & Discovery

- Home overview: balance, pending earnings, active/upcoming campaigns at a glance
- Actions required: reminders for anything due (e.g. post content after a visit)
- AI recommendations: ranked by creator profile title + how active a business is on Sparkr (e.g. food bloggers see busy restaurants first)
- Featured campaigns: businesses pay to feature for up to 30 days, matched to creator type, shown at top
- Search & filters: location, category, compensation, platform, follower requirements, campaign dates
- **Not included**: influencer posts on the Sparkr feed, follower verification/audits, brand deals outside Sparkr

## Feature 22 — Campaign Management

- My Campaigns: tracks invited → applied → hired → scheduled → posted → completed
- Calendar of booked visits + saved campaigns for later
- Wallet: paid + pending earnings; paid from the business's Sparkr wallet within 72 hours of post approval
- Messaging: chat opens once a business accepts the influencer for a campaign
- Disputes: either party can raise a dispute if terms aren't met (e.g. agreed offer/payment not given)
- **Not included**: Sparkr guaranteeing payments, contract/tax handling, messaging before acceptance

## Feature 23 — Live Posts & Links

- Post directly on own socials; due 10 days after visit; **no business approval needed first**
- Revision requests: business can ask for caption/hashtag changes only, via in-app messaging; no response within 7 days = automatic approval
- Posting is from any account type, no draft upload needed
- Live link as proof: influencer pastes the live link; Sparkr checks the account where APIs allow, otherwise business confirms
- Influencer codes: unique code/link per hired influencer; business sees redemptions
- **Not included**: posting to social media through Sparkr itself, clickable links in IG/TikTok captions, customer details shown to influencers, business approval before posting
